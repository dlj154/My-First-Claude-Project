About Me

I'm a product leader with a proven track record of building and scaling SaaS products from the ground up. Serving as Head of Product at Charted, I led teams that launched three new products, grew software revenue from $5.4M to $10M+, and established product-market fit for a global payments product. My experience spans fintech, payments, and B2B SaaS, with expertise in product strategy, go-to-market execution, and turning ambitious ideas into revenue-generating solutions.

Before entering product management, I served as a Captain in the United States Marine Corps, leading teams in high-stakes environments. I hold an MBA from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business and bring a unique blend of strategic thinking, operational discipline, and customer-focused innovation to every project.

When I'm not building products, you'll find me playing tuba with in the Triangle Brass Band, coaching CrossFit in my garage gym, or sharing insights on accounts payable and payments through speaking engagements and thought leadership.
